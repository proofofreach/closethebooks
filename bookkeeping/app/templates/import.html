Formats and optional bank connections

QBO (QuickBooks Web Connect) contains bank activity. Use Accounting history for a company ledger. PayPal CSV needs the PayPal activity parser in account settings, including fees. PDF support is currently QuickBooks Checking.

For other CSV layouts, upload a file and choose Map CSV in the inbox. Choose date order and amount direction, then review all rows before saving.

Plaid and Teller are optional advanced feeds configured from the command line. Plaid developer enrollment can require identity and business checks. Start with files to avoid that dependency.
